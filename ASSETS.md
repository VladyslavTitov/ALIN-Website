# Asset provenance

Original raster assets were generated for this project using the built-in image-generation tool. Full prompts are in IMAGE_PROMPTS.json. The images are fictional concepts, not real clinic photography or authentic staff portraits. The generated logo symbol is paired with a live-text ALIN wordmark.

- consultation: fictional dentist and adult patient talking at eye level
- portrait: fictional female dentist in teal practice clothing
- reception: fictional oak, warm-white and sage reception
- treatment: fictional bright treatment room
- logo: original tooth/leaf symbol

Resizing, alpha-bound trimming of the logo and WebP compression were applied for delivery. No images were sourced from another dental practice.

Manrope: Google Fonts / Mikhail Sharanda, SIL Open Font License; public/fonts/Manrope-OFL.txt.
Source Sans 3: Adobe / Google Fonts, SIL Open Font License; public/fonts/SourceSans3-OFL.txt.
Lucide icons and installed UI packages retain their upstream package licences in their dependencies.

Clinical reference links are displayed on treatment pages. The clinical copy remains a draft pending review by the actual dentist. No stock-image or paid-plugin licence is required by the implemented design. Third-party service subscriptions are separate.
